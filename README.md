# RoleReady
### From Opportunity Discovery to Placement Readiness

**RoleReady** is a student-focused internship and placement preparation platform designed to bring opportunity discovery, eligibility checking, skill-gap identification, and application tracking into one place.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Students often search for internships and placement opportunities across multiple company websites and recruitment platforms. Managing eligibility criteria, application deadlines, required skills, and preparation resources separately can become time-consuming and confusing.

RoleReady aims to simplify this process through a unified platform.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Provide a centralized platform for internship and placement opportunities.
- Help students check their eligibility for opportunities.
- Compare student skills with job requirements.
- Identify missing skills and areas for improvement.
- Support placement preparation through relevant resources.
- Help students track their applications.

## ✨ Key Features

1. **Student Profile Management**  
   Maintain academic details, skills, interests, and career preferences.

2. **Opportunity Discovery**  
   Explore internship and placement opportunities.

3. **Eligibility Checking**  
   Check opportunities against academic and other mandatory criteria.

4. **Skill Matching**  
   Compare student skills with the skills required by an opportunity.

5. **Skill-Gap Analysis**  
   Identify missing skills to help students understand what they need to improve.

6. **Placement Preparation**  
   Provide relevant learning and practice resources.

7. **Application Tracking**  
   Maintain application details and track application progress.

## ⚙️ System Workflow

1. Students create their profiles and add their academic details and skills.
2. Students explore available opportunities.
3. The system checks mandatory eligibility criteria.
4. The matching process compares student skills with opportunity requirements.
5. Missing skills are identified to support preparation.
6. Students can maintain and track their applications.

## 🧠 Matching Approach

RoleReady uses a rule-based approach to compare student profiles with opportunity requirements.

**Skill Match Percentage:**

`(Number of Matched Skills / Total Required Skills) × 100`

The system can also identify missing skills, helping students understand the gap between their current skills and opportunity requirements.

## 🚀 Project Scope

RoleReady focuses on supporting informed placement preparation. It does not guarantee placements or claim fully automated job discovery. Opportunity availability and application status depend on the data sources and updates available to the platform.
